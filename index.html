Machine Learning for Physical Systems: demos

Interactive demos for the lecture, winter semester 2026/27. Each runs in the browser, also on a phone, and needs no installation.

  • Fit or overfit?
    Lecture 1 · short link https://alhermann.github.io/mlps-demos/go/d01
    Fit a straight line by hand, then find out how flexible a curve should be to predict new measurements.
  • Watch a network learn
    Lecture 2 · short link https://alhermann.github.io/mlps-demos/go/d02
    One bent line, then many: watch a small neural network learn how rubber stiffens, forward and back.
